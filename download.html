📱 Install on iPhone / iPad

No App Store needed
1 Open Safari on your iPhone (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2 Go to aeskills.github.io/AssociatePulse or tap the blue button above
3 Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom of Safari
4 Scroll down the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen" ➕
5 Tap "Add" in the top right — the AssociatePulse app with the AP icon will appear on your home screen!
